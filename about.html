ABOUT THE PRACTICE

Built for systems where failure matters.

Scientific facilities and critical infrastructure require engineering that respects determinism, operations, maintainability and the mission behind the machine.

Critical systems for advanced scientific facilities.

Control Lattice Systems is an independent engineering practice led by Rob Rainer. The practice is built around more than 20 years of experience with scientific facilities, controls, advanced instrumentation, data-center infrastructure, technical leadership and systems integration.

Engineering complexity. Enabling discovery.

Our approach starts with the physical system and the people who operate it. Controls, software, data, infrastructure and documentation are treated as parts of one operating environment. New technology is useful only when it makes that environment clearer, more reliable or more capable.

Evidence before hype.

Public technical work—including the Source Stability Observer and accelerator timing digital-twin work—makes parts of the approach inspectable. We distinguish demonstrated capability from commercial deployment and avoid unsupported performance claims.
